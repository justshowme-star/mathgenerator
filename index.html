Coordinate grid

Add lines or points, label them, then set up the axes and grid. The graph updates as you type.

1Lines and points▾

Type an equation, inequality or point. The symbol buttons insert at the cursor in whichever box you last clicked.

y = 2x + 1 y ≥ x − 3 x = 4 x² + y² = 9 f(x) = x² A(2, 3), B(−1, 4)

Press Piecewise for a fill-in editor, or type one line: f(x) = {x < 0: x + 1, 0 ≤ x < 2: x², 3}. A last entry with no condition covers everything else. Labels show it as a proper piecewise function.

Limit a graph with braces: y = x + 1 {−2 ≤ x < 4}. Use ^ for powers, / for fractions and sqrt( ) for roots. Letters like a, h and k become sliders.

Click the colored circle to show or hide a row. Press and hold it to change the color. For several points, name them in the label box as A; B; C.

Use the ⋯ button on a row to duplicate it, move it up or down, delete it, or mark it as answer key only (hidden in the student copy). The toolbar switches between the answer key and student copy, and between color and black and white.

2Axes▾

From and To set the visible range. Step is the distance between gridlines. You can type fractions and pi, like pi/2. If the numbers on the axes look small, raise Axis number size.

You can also drag the graph to move it, and use Ctrl + scroll or the + and − buttons to zoom. These boxes update when you let go.

3Grid▾

Choose the gridline color and thickness, add minor lines, and change the block size (how big each square is).

4Titles and text▾

Titles are optional. Powers work here too: x^2 shows as x².

Working with the graph

Drag to move the view. Hold Ctrl (Cmd on a Mac) and scroll to zoom, or use the + and − buttons. After you click the graph, plain scrolling, the arrow keys and + and − also work. The view snaps to the grid when you let go. On a touch screen, tap ✥ to drag the graph; otherwise your finger scrolls the page.

Drag a label to place it (double-click it to reset). Click a line or point to edit its row.

Ctrl+Z undoes and Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes. Your work saves automatically in this browser.
